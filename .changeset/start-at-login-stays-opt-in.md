With Start at login on, the menu bar app now comes back on its own after macOS or a memory-pressure pass kills it. Start at login stays your choice: upgrading does not turn it on, turning it off keeps it off, and choosing Quit from the menu still keeps the app closed. If you already had Start at login on and macOS asks you to allow directa in System Settings after the upgrade, directa keeps starting at login the old way until you do.
